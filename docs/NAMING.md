# App name — candidates

> Status: **not decided** (last updated 2026-11-14). Working title: "Color Picker". Decide before the first Google Play upload (see "Constraints").

## Constraints

- **Google Play title**: max 30 characters. Plan: `<Brand>: Color Picker`.
- **Short description** (max 80 characters) carries the positioning, e.g. *"Color picker for artists & designers: name, explore and save colors"*.
- **App ID** (`applicationId`): `io.github.luigipionargiso.<brand>`. It can be changed freely during development, but becomes **permanent after the first upload to Google Play**. Until then the project uses the working ID `io.github.luigipionargiso.colorpicker`.
- **Avoid**: names already used by other color apps (Play Store policy on misleading names / impersonation). "Color Picker for Artists" is excluded for this reason: it is the name of an existing paid iOS app by artist Jasmina Susak ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1380387828)). The phrase is still used in the short description.

## Notes

- Claude's favorites: **Colorlore** (matches the names-and-stories feature, likely available), **Palette Knife** (instantly says "for painters"), **Ochre** (short, elegant, works as an icon).
- Probably crowded (common words or existing brands): Swatchbook, Easel, Sfumato, Ultramarine.
